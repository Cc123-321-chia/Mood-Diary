Mood Diary Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mood Diary collects and uses information provided by users.

1. Information We Collect

Mood Diary collects account information such as names and email addresses. Users may also provide diary entries, mood selections, and uploaded images or videos.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used to provide account access and support diary management features.

3. Account Security

Users should choose strong, unique passwords, keep their login information private, and log out when using shared devices.

4. Data Access and Management

Users can manage their diary entries through the available application features. Access to information is subject to the application's implemented permissions.

5. Privacy Questions

For questions about personal information, users should contact the application administrator.
